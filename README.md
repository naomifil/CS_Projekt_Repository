## Concept
Business Case: 
Personal Air Quality Health Advisor
 
People with asthma, allergies, or respiratory conditions have no easy way to decide when it's safe to exercise or go
outside. AirSense gives a personalized daily risk score based on their health profile, local air quality, and weather 
data

With the help of machine learning we provide a forecast of air quality based on weather forecast, evaluate the data
and return a risk score to the user
